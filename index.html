Debt Repayment Calculator

Compare Snowball (smallest outstanding balance first) and Avalanche (highest annual interest first). All existing EMIs continue to be paid. Any monthly extra amount, plus EMI amounts freed after a loan closes, is redirected to the next priority loan.

The extra amount comes from the “Extra Amount Available for Debt Repayment” field above. You can test with ₹1,000. This calculator does not change your real loan or bank payments.

Estimates only: monthly interest is approximated as outstanding balance × annual rate ÷ 12. Actual lender calculations may differ due to daily interest, fees, rate changes, payment dates and prepayment rules. A loan with an EMI too low to cover its interest will be flagged.
